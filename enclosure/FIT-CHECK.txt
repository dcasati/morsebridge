MORSEBRIDGE ENCLOSURE - V6 FIT-CHECK PROTOTYPE

Reference: user-supplied obj_1_Body1.stl and obj_2_Body2.stl.
These originals are not modified. This is an editable rebuild inspired by their
rounded shape, not a mesh patch or compatible spare lid.
Print BOTH new parts together; do not combine the new base with the original lid.

PROPOSED LAYOUT (all measurements in mm)
Revised request: 45 long x 24 wide x 16 high OUTSIDE, including the lid.
Base height: 14.4. Wall, floor and lid roof thickness: 1.6.
Nominal cavity: 41.8 x 20.8, before the jack cradle and corner radii.
Floor-to-lid clearance: 12.8; clips/lip occupy local portions of this space.
USB-C at one short end, paddle jack at the opposite short end.
Board envelope: 23.5 x 18, with no pin headers.
Jack housing reserve: 14 x 12 x 5, based conservatively on supplied drawing.
PCB support pads and USB-end guides removed as requested; jack cradle retained.
4.0 mm nominal longitudinal gap between component envelopes.
Each component has 0.15 mm nominal clearance at its adjacent end wall.
The width is unchanged; this revision adds length and height, not lateral space.
This is not a tolerance-qualified fit. Measure real parts including solder/wires.
Wire route is beside/under the components. Keep wire loops and jack metal away
from the antenna as far as practicable; test BLE range in the assembled case.

THIS IS NOT YET A GUARANTEED FIT
The supplied XIAO case has an approximately 18 mm-wide cavity at mid-height.
That gives the 18 mm Waveshare board no width clearance, so scaling or using
the original internal mounts is inappropriate.

The USB cutout follows the ORIGINAL case's rounded profile: 9.5 wide x 3.4 high
with 1.5 corner radii. These dimensions were measured from end-wall sections
of both original case halves. This replaces the oversized 12 x 6 rectangle.
Its centre stays at 5.4 above the bottom of this case. The PCB support pads
and guides are now removed; the opening was NOT lowered. Confirm port elevation,
USB connector overhang, and the actual cable's plastic overmould.
The jack wall opening is ROUND, exactly 6.0 nominal diameter as requested.
This is for the jack's outer barrel, NOT the 3.5 mm plug. No extra print-fit
allowance has been added: a nominal 6 mm barrel may require hole compensation
or careful finishing. Verify protrusion, housing and terminal lengths.
The jack cradle is lowered to 0.4 above the floor because the supplied photo
shows its terminals facing UP. There is 7.4 above the nominal 5 mm housing,
including a provisional 2.5 solder/terminal allowance plus 4.9 lid clearance.
Solder joints, bent terminals and insulation must fit within that allowance.
The board's nominal underside remains at 3.2 above the outside bottom for
alignment checks only. With no built-in PCB supports, provide insulated
retention at the correct height; do not rely on the USB plug to hold the PCB.
The wide lid slot is replaced with a ROUND 3.0 mm LED viewing hole.
Its centre is now X=13.5 from the OUTSIDE USB-end face and Y=12 from the
outside long side, moved 1 mm toward USB-C as requested.
Measure the actual fitted LED before printing the final lid and adjust LED_X
and LED_Y in prototype.py. This opening is not a sealed light pipe.
The USB-C opening is a rounded rectangle; the jack opening at the opposite end
is circular. The 6 mm jack bore and 3 mm LED hole are unchanged from V3.
BOOT/RESET access is by removing the lid.

LID AND RETENTION
V6 replaces the plain locating skirt with two positive snap clips, one on
each long side. The locating lip has 0.4 clearance per side (formerly 0.25)
and is only 1.2 deep (formerly 2.0) to reduce insertion friction.
Each clip is 5 wide, 0.8 thick and 6.4 long, with a sloped entry ramp and
0.45 nominal engagement in a recessed side-wall catch.
The lip is interrupted around each clip so the clip can flex independently.
Small 3 x 1 side access slots allow clips to be pressed inward for removal.
The clip bottoms sit above the nominal PCB envelope, not beneath it.

PRINT BOTH V6 PARTS. Older lids lack these catches/clips and are not compatible.
This is a geometric snap-fit design, not a physically qualified closure:
material, layer adhesion and actual extrusion determine force and durability.
The prototype was printed in PLA+; PETG is also an option.
Dry-fit the empty case first, without electronics. Press gently above both
clips until they engage; do not force it.
For removal, with USB disconnected, push the clips inward through the side
slots using a small blunt nonconductive tool while gently lifting the lid.
Do not drive a tool through the case toward the electronics.
If it binds or does not latch, check lip clearance, pocket cleanliness and
print dimensions before changing LID_GAP or HOOK_OUTER_Y in the generator.
The jack has a low saddle and side guides, with space above for soldered leads.
The earlier rear stop and lid rib are removed to avoid component interference.
The lid skirt is relieved above the jack. Verify plug insertion and removal
loads; soldered pins must not serve as mechanical retention.
The four PCB pads and two short USB-end side guides remain absent.
PCB retention is not provided or qualified.

FIRST PRINT
Use 0.4 nozzle, 0.16 or 0.20 layers, 4 walls, and 15-20% infill as a starting
point. PLA+ was used for the prototype; PETG is an alternative. Clip durability
depends on the filament formulation, print settings and layer adhesion.
Avoid leaving PLA/PLA+ in a hot vehicle.
STLs are already oriented: base floor down; lid outer face down, lip/clips up.
Inspect USB/jack bridges, catch roofs and hook shoulders in the slicer; use
local removable supports if needed. Completely clean the moving clip gaps.
No printer command is issued by this generator.

CHECK BEFORE POWER
Disconnect USB while fitting. Insulate every soldered joint and terminal.
Check that the jack housing, not its terminals, takes plug insertion force.
Confirm the lid does not push on components, antenna, buttons or solder joints.
Confirm USB plug seating, both paddle inputs, LED visibility and BLE range.

LENGTH AND HEIGHT INTERPRETATION
V6 supersedes the previous 38 internal-length / 8.2 internal-height request.
The new 45 x 24 x 16 dimensions describe the entire assembled enclosure.
The lid STL is 8 tall because it includes inward-pointing clips; those overlap
the base's height when assembled. Do not add the separate STL heights together.
V1 through V5 outputs remain unchanged in their separate Downloads folders.
Earlier exports may retain the historical Morse Paddle name.
Current files: morsebridge_base_45x24x16_v6.stl and morsebridge_lid_45x24x16_v6.stl.
The published STL pair and mesh-checks.json are in enclosure/v6/ in the repository.
