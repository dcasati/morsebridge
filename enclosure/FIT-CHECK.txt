MORSEBRIDGE ENCLOSURE - V4 FIT-CHECK PROTOTYPE

Reference: user-supplied obj_1_Body1.stl and obj_2_Body2.stl.
These originals are not modified. This is an editable rebuild inspired by their
rounded shape, not a mesh patch or compatible spare lid.
Print BOTH new parts together; do not combine the new base with the original lid.

PROPOSED LAYOUT (all measurements in mm)
Requested length interpreted as 38 INTERNAL, not external.
The supplied caliper photo reads 38.0 across the laid-out assembly.
Requested minimum height interpreted as 8.2 from floor to underside of lid.
Outside: 41.2 long x 24 wide x 11.4 high, including lid.
Base height: 9.8. Wall, floor and lid roof thickness: 1.6.
Nominal cavity: 38 x 20.8, before internal supports and corner radii.
Floor-to-lid clearance: 8.2; supports/skirt occupy local portions of this space.
USB-C at one short end, paddle jack at the opposite short end.
Board envelope: 23.5 x 18, with no pin headers.
Jack housing reserve: 14 x 12 x 5, based conservatively on supplied drawing.
Separate board/jack supports, only 0.2 mm nominal longitudinal gap.
Each component has 0.15 mm nominal clearance at its adjacent end wall.
This is a tight nominal layout, not a tolerance-qualified fit. Measure real parts.
Wire route is beside/under the components. Keep wire loops and jack metal away
from the antenna as far as practicable; test BLE range in the assembled case.

THIS IS NOT YET A GUARANTEED FIT
The supplied XIAO case has an approximately 18 mm-wide cavity at mid-height.
That gives the 18 mm Waveshare board no width clearance, so scaling or using
the original internal mounts is inappropriate.

The USB cutout follows the ORIGINAL case's rounded profile: 9.5 wide x 3.4 high
with 1.5 corner radii. These dimensions were measured from end-wall sections
of both original case halves. This replaces the oversized 12 x 6 rectangle.
Its centre stays at 5.4 above the bottom of this case, since the Waveshare
board supports differ from the original XIAO mounts. Confirm port elevation,
USB connector overhang, and the actual cable's plastic overmould.
The jack wall opening is ROUND, exactly 6.0 nominal diameter as requested.
This is for the jack's outer barrel, NOT the 3.5 mm plug. No extra print-fit
allowance has been added: a nominal 6 mm barrel may require hole compensation
or careful finishing. Verify protrusion, housing and terminal lengths.
The jack cradle is lowered to 0.4 above the floor because the supplied photo
shows its terminals facing UP. There is 2.8 above the nominal 5 mm housing,
including a provisional 2.5 solder/terminal allowance plus 0.3 lid clearance.
Solder joints, bent terminals and insulation must fit within that allowance.
Board ledges may need moving to avoid components or soldered GPIO wires.
The wide lid slot is replaced with a ROUND 3.0 mm LED viewing hole.
Its centre is provisionally X=14.5 from the OUTSIDE USB-end face and Y=12 from
the outside long side: the old slot centre, NOT a verified LED measurement.
Measure the actual fitted LED before printing the final lid and adjust LED_X
and LED_Y in prototype.py. This opening is not a sealed light pipe.
The USB-C opening is a rounded rectangle; the jack opening at the opposite end
is circular. The 6 mm jack bore and 3 mm LED hole are unchanged from V3.
BOOT/RESET access is by removing the lid.

LID AND RETENTION
The lid has a locating skirt with 0.25 clearance per side. It is deliberately
NOT represented as a snap-fit or mechanically secure production closure.
Use removable tape for the first bench fit; do not rely on this lid to retain
the assembly during field use. Final snap tabs or fastening require a fit test.
The jack has a low saddle and side guides, with space above for soldered leads.
The earlier rear stop and lid rib are removed to avoid component interference.
The lid skirt is relieved above the jack. Verify plug insertion and removal
loads; soldered pins must not serve as mechanical retention.
The PCB has edge supports and side guides but no qualified top clamp yet.

FIRST PRINT
Use 0.4 nozzle, 0.16 or 0.20 layers, 4 walls, and 15-20% infill as a starting
point. PLA is suitable for a bench fit; PETG is preferable for a tougher final
case after recalibrating clearances. Avoid leaving PLA in a hot vehicle.
STLs are already oriented: base floor down; lid outer face down, skirt up.
Inspect the slicer's USB/jack opening bridges; add local supports if needed.
No printer command is issued by this generator.

CHECK BEFORE POWER
Disconnect USB while fitting. Insulate every soldered joint and terminal.
Check that the jack housing, not its terminals, takes plug insertion force.
Confirm the lid does not push on components, antenna, buttons or solder joints.
Confirm USB plug seating, both paddle inputs, LED visibility and BLE range.

LENGTH AND HEIGHT INTERPRETATION
38 external length would leave only 34.8 internally with these walls, too short
for the nominal 37.5 combined board/jack length. V4 therefore uses 38 inside.
8.2 external height would not leave 8.2 for parts. V4 uses 8.2 clear height,
plus the 1.6 floor and 1.6 lid, for 11.4 overall.
